## About the CAC

The Community Accountability Commission (CAC) is a group of neighbors, shelter operators, and service providers who meet regularly to ensure the shelter operates in a way that is accountable to the surrounding community. The CAC reviews operations, addresses concerns, and works to foster communication between the shelter and the neighborhood.
